# Andie Gong

I'm an R2/PGY-3 diagnostic radiology resident at Mass General Brigham, Brigham and Women's Hospital in Boston. I earned my undergraduate degree in physics at UC Berkeley and my medical degree at UCLA, where I had the fortune of working with the Center for Computer Vision and Imaging Biomarkers (CVIB). My professional interests include neuroradiology, thoracic imaging, medical education, and computational research in automating oncologic treatment response assessment.

I am proudly queer, non-binary (they/them), and first-generation Chinese-American. I spend most of my free time  rock climbing and hanging out with my friends and wonderful wife, Regine. I also enjoy reading, journaling, and listening to string music.
